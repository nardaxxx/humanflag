## To translate one page

1. Open `<page>-<lang>.html` and replace the English text with the translation.
2. Delete the `<p class="pending">` line near the top and the scaffold comment above it.
3. Tell me, and I add the URL to `sitemap.xml` — it stays out until the text is real,
   so Google is never given the same English page under eight addresses.

The language bar, the internal navigation and the links from each translated
home page are already wired. Nothing else needs touching.
