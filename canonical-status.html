Canonical identifiers and authoritative deposits for the Human Flag Association corpus

Which version governs

Where a document is deposited on Zenodo, the Zenodo deposit is the authoritative version. Each document is identified here by its concept DOI, which resolves to the current deposit. Citations should use the concept DOI.

Copies held elsewhere — including SSRN, this website and any circulated file — may lag behind the Zenodo deposit. Where the text of another copy diverges from the Zenodo record, the Zenodo record governs. One document departs from this arrangement, and it is stated explicitly below.

The exception

Paper III is deposited in DOCX, not PDF. The authoritative deposit of HF SIGNAL 01 under 10.5281/zenodo.21183137 is a Word document. It is the version that governs, notwithstanding the format. Any PDF rendering of the specification in circulation is a derived copy and carries no independent authority.

On stability. Concept DOIs remain stable as deposits are revised and resolve to the current record. This page states where each document lives and which copy governs; it makes no claim as to adoption, legal status or operational validation of any document in the corpus.
