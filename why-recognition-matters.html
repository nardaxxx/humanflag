A note on recognition

Corpus Guide Document status Why Human Flag What we do How we act Symbol Why Arrestability Donations Contact 📄 Paper I 📄 Paper II 📄 Paper III 📝 Technical Notes Statement to the CCW GGE

Harm can continue when it is not recognized, named or heard. The person who suffers may remain silent, may not understand what is happening, or may be unable to express it. Without recognition, the harm can continue without being answered.

“Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves.” — Proverbs 31:8

Human Flag begins from the same human premise. A person may surrender, ask for violence to stop, or cease to be a lawful target. But if that change cannot be expressed or recognized, it cannot affect what happens next.

Recognition is what allows restraint to begin.

This is a human reflection, not a source of international humanitarian law.
